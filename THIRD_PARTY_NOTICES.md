# Third-party sources and software

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants apply to the authors' own contributions only.
Third-party raw records, facility-coordinate databases, geographic rasters,
administrative boundaries and assessment PDFs are retrieved from their providers.
No OSM database, CNES raw file, SAEH admission row, SIH DBC, GHSL/friction raster,
SARA PDF, personal information or library binary is included in this repository.

Providers include DATASUS (SIH/CNES/SIGTAP), Mexico's national health-information
services (SAEH/CLUES), the European Commission GHSL, OpenStreetMap contributors,
Natural Earth, the Malaria Atlas Project, WorldPop and World Bank.
OpenStreetMap source databases are governed by ODbL; author aggregate results
are not a substitute source-database licence. Reusers must comply with each
provider's actual terms. A public download URL alone is not a licence grant.
Some inherited release identifiers and licence records remain incomplete;
those files have deliberately not been redistributed or labelled CC BY 4.0.

Python/C dependencies retain their respective licences and are installed through
package managers; they are not vendored. The publication plotting code is
author code. Standard licence texts are reproduced as licence documents.
